## Notes and limits

- The oracle cannot be moved on testnet. The guardian steps set the policy floor above the position's current ratio, so the keeper acts because the contract's own planner (`preview`) says to. That is a legitimate trigger, but it is not a price crash.
- The seller is also the fee recipient on this deployment (the deployer), so the fee is shown through the MUSD `Transfer` logs and the `Sold` event rather than as a change in someone else's balance.
- The keeper in step "tops the position up" is the same code that runs on the GitHub schedule ([`keeper/src/keeper.ts`](../keeper/src/keeper.ts)); it was pointed at this vault and sent the transaction.
- The first keeper attempt at the ratio-floor top-up FAILED on chain: tx 0x1002b8e119c9d86d7bb25f1f70337a892ef9aa0c059eab68e584e43a8b1d2e60 (block 16612677) ran out of gas at a limit of 25,584. The node's eth_estimateGas answered about 21,300 for execute(), while the call needs about 5.6M. No funds moved and the reserve was untouched. The keeper now never sends under a 6M gas floor (Mezo charges at least half of a limit, which at 6M is still under 0.000000001 BTC), and the successful attempt below is the retry.
- preview before the keeper: TopUpBtc amount 2655839984116575 tip 10000000000000
- At 135% the position sat more than 100 troves from the front of the redemption queue on testnet (the walk covered 100 steps and 98 eligible troves worth 271,804 MUSD without reaching it), so the first defense attempt returned BufferUnknown and, correctly, did not act. The ratio was then lowered to move it forward; see the steps below.
- The first redemption-defense attempt by the keeper FAILED on chain: tx 0xae438464f0d83b18331933235c62cc93580f12caef1c0bcd2e24b7f6ea74722c (block 16612964) used 5,584,423 gas of a 6M limit and ran out of gas, because the keeper sent no sorted-list hints and the defense adds a 100-step buffer walk (about 7.3M gas in total). No funds moved. The keeper now computes hints from the planner's amount before sending, and the successful defense below used them. Separately, one of my own proof-script transactions (the defense setPolicy) was sent with a gas limit taken from the node's estimate, which was far too low (23,092, under the chain's data-gas floor), and the node rejected it before inclusion; the script now floors every limit at 6M.
